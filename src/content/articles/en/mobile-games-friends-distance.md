## How to actually play with friends from afar

Getting the game running is one thing, making it smooth is another. A few small tweaks make the difference between "let's play again" and "my connection dropped".

- **Voice chat wins**: Use Discord, WhatsApp calls, or a group call. Text works, but yelling over Among Us doesn't hit the same way.
- **Check NAT/region**: Some games prefer same region lobbies. If you're getting long queue times, try switching regions if available.
- **WiFi vs mobile data**: WiFi is usually more stable. If someone is on mobile, 4G/5G usually works fine for party games.
- **Avoid screen sharing lag**: Don't stream while playing competitive games; it eats bandwidth.
- **Quick rooms**: Create private rooms/lobbies whenever possible instead of public matchmaking if you want to keep the group together.

## The games worth your time (deep dive)

### Among Us
Still unbeatable for chaos. It's free on mobile, easy to join, rounds are short (5-10 min), and the drama writes itself. Best with 6-10 people.

### Gartic Phone
Think broken telephone meets drawing. One person writes a phrase, next draws it, next guesses, etc. The end results are absurd. Requires zero gaming skill.

### Skribbl.io
A classic drawing guessing game. Works well on mobile browser too, so nobody needs to install if they don't want to.

### BombSquad
Local-multiplayer energy turned online-ish. Explosions, ragdoll physics, pure mayhem. Great stress relief.

### Minecraft: Bedrock Edition
If your group likes projects, this is your long-term pick. Build a base, go mining, or just troll each other. Crossplay works across platforms.

### Roblox
Not one game, a platform. Find a game everyone likes (obby, tycoons, murder mysteries, simulators). Good variety for mixed tastes.

## FAQ

**Do all these games need accounts?**
Most do (to save progress/friends), but joining lobbies is usually fast. Roblox, Among Us, Skribbl.io are easy to get into.

**Do they work across iOS and Android?**
Yes, the vast majority are cross-platform between Android and iOS. Minecraft Bedrock, Among Us, Gartic Phone, Roblox all work fine.

**Can I play with people in other countries?**
Absolutely. Most of these use global matchmaking or invite links. Latency varies, but party games tolerate it better than twitch shooters.

**Are they really free?**
Yes, all listed are free-to-play. Some have cosmetics/ads, but you don't need to pay to play with friends.
