**GTA VI is the launch that has half the internet looking at Rockstar again: Jason and Lucia, the fictional state of Leonida and a Vice City inspired by Florida.** Here is, without the noise and without filler, everything you need to know to arrive prepared.

> Short answer: the official date is November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The campaign follows Jason and Lucia in Leonida and, if you play on PC, you will have to wait. It is a GTA, so the story stands on its own and the online is a separate world.

Rockstar does not do normal launches, and this one least of all. GTA V sold more than 200 million copies and its online is still alive over a decade later. GTA VI is not coming to compete with that: it is coming to beat it. This guide is split into blocks so you can read what interests you and skip what you already know.

## What GTA VI is (and why it is not just another GTA)

GTA VI is the next main entry in the saga, developed by Rockstar Games and published by Rockstar and Take-Two. It is not a spin-off or an expansion: it is the next numbered chapter, the first since 2013.

The big difference is the approach. In GTA V you controlled three protagonists who took turns. Here Rockstar has built the campaign around a couple, Jason and Lucia, with a relationship that weighs as much as the heists. It is the story of two people who need each other to get ahead, and that changes the tone: less superhero spectacle and more drama about people getting into ever-bigger trouble.

Rockstar has pitched it as its biggest and most ambitious world to date. With what a GTA costs, that line is not empty marketing: it means years of development and the highest budget the industry can remember.

## Leonida: the Vice City that eats the map

The setting is not simply "Vice City": it is **Leonida**, a fictional state inspired by Florida that contains Vice City as its main city. That allows something the previous GTAs did not have so close at hand: moving from a saturated urban centre to swamps, beaches, endless roads and lost towns without leaving the same map.

If you have seen the trailers, you already know what it smells like: neon, heat, expensive cars and a sense of paradise with unpaid bills. Leonida is Florida with the volume turned up, and that sets up the saga's classic contrasts: millionaires and people on the edge of eviction sharing the same street.

For the player, the consequence is simple: there is a lot to explore and very little that is just set dressing. The joy of a GTA has always been getting lost, and with a map like this, getting lost is the plan.

## Jason and Lucia: the couple that changes the formula

Jason and Lucia are the two protagonists. The reference everyone has made is Bonnie and Clyde: a couple who commit crimes together and whose relationship drives the story, not decorates it.

That has two effects. The first is narrative: the campaign can tell a story of love and betrayal at the same time, without one covering for the other. The second is gameplay: the two characters justify different ways of playing, and the couple invites you to think of every job as teamwork.

Do not expect the classic "silent protagonist who does what he is told". Here there are two voices, two ways of seeing the same disaster and, probably, two ways of ruining it all.

## Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series and the PC question

At launch, GTA VI is available on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**. If you own a current-generation console, you are in.

The PC situation is the usual one with Rockstar: the computer version does not arrive on day one. The company has repeated the pattern with its big releases and, for now, there is no locked date for PC. If your only option is a computer, the sensible move is not to buy a console in a hurry: wait for official confirmation and, in the meantime, catch up on the saga.

On editions and prices, always check your console's store or the official website on the day you buy. It is the only place where the terms are up to date and where you will not get surprises with downloadable content or virtual currency.

## How to prepare before you start playing

A GTA is not played in a hurry, but it is prepared. Four things before you turn on the console:

- **Plenty of free space.** GTA VI is a huge game. Do not settle for the minimum the store asks for: leave room for patches and so you do not have to delete games halfway through the campaign.
- **Updated console.** Patches usually land in the first days. Update the system and the game before you start so you do not hit a download in the middle of a mission.
- **Headphones.** The soundtrack and the ambient sound are part of the show, and in an open world listening warns you about things you cannot see.
- **Watch out for spoilers.** During launch week, the internet is a minefield. Mute keywords on social media and avoid "all the endings" videos until you finish.

And a real recommendation: if you can, play the first hours without looking at guides. You only get a first impression of a GTA once.

## The first hours: how to start on the right foot

- **Do not rush the story.** The game will push you toward the next mission. Resist a little and explore: the best GTA moments are usually off the main line.
- **Learn the wanted system.** Understand how the police chase you and how to lose them. It is the difference between an epic escape and dying in an alley.
- **Invest in the basics.** A good car and some saved money spare you frustration in the first hours. Do not spend everything customising the first vehicle you see.
- **Tune the controls and accessibility.** GTA VI packs a lot of options: sensitivity, aim assist, subtitles and more. Take five minutes to set them your way; it shows.
- **Save often.** In an open world, a perfect plan falls apart over something silly. Saving is free.

## What about online?

GTA VI's campaign is single-player. Multiplayer is another story and Rockstar has not revealed all its details, so be suspicious of anyone promising exact dates or modes.

What is certain: GTA Online, the one from GTA V, is still a monster with millions of players and years of content. If you come from there, you will not find a desert: you will find a huge community waiting for what comes next. When GTA VI's online arrives, it will arrive to stay.

## Frequently asked questions

**What platforms is GTA VI on?**
PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is a new-generation console release; versions for older consoles are not planned.

**When does GTA VI come out on PC?**
Rockstar has not confirmed a date for PC. Historically, its big games reach computers later, so the prudent thing is to wait for an official announcement.

**Do I need to have played the previous GTAs?**
No. Every numbered entry tells its own story with new characters. You can start with GTA VI without missing anything important.

**Is it suitable for minors?**
No. Like the rest of the saga, it is a game for adults: violence, language and content for over-18s. Respect the age rating.

**Can I play it alone?**
Yes. The campaign is designed for one player. Multiplayer is a separate mode you do not need to touch to finish the story.

**How long is GTA VI?**
Rockstar has not confirmed an official length. Being a GTA, expect dozens of hours of campaign and many more if you get lost on the map doing side missions.

> If you take away one idea, make it this: GTA VI is not about finishing fast, it is about living in Leonida for a while. Get in the car, turn on the radio and let the map do the rest.
